# Novel audit, wrong assignment: realigning "Did It Really Forget?"

The team's current project does **not** meet the assignment card as written. It reproduces nothing from Medical SAM3, uses SAM ViT-B rather than SAM 3, stays inside one modality (colonoscopy, whose Kvasir-SEG data is already in Medical SAM3's training set), and has no text prompts, no ultrasound or dermoscopy, and no prompt-selection protocol. In isolation, though, the unlearning audit has a defensible research gap. Corrective unlearning in segmentation, a spatially localized internal residual audit, pixel-level prediction of where an artifact re-emerges, and prompt-driven re-emergence in a promptable segmentation model all turned up no matching prior work in this session's searches. The surrounding ideas are well published: "unlearning is suppression, not deletion", relearning attacks, and model-level audits that forecast recovery. The card's own premise is also weaker than it looks. Medical SAM3 trains on seven ultrasound datasets and ISIC 2018 dermoscopy, and it already reports zero-shot results on external ultrasound (TN3K, HC18) and dermoscopy (PH2) sets. Several 2026 papers have also covered point, box and text prompts for SAM 3 on those modalities. So the card supplies the skeleton but little novelty, and the unlearning twist supplies the novelty but currently ignores the skeleton. The fix is a merged project: reproduce Medical SAM3's external table, fine-tune it onto genuinely held-out ultrasound and dermoscopy data with corrupted masks, apply corrective unlearning, and measure how well the forgetting holds up across prompt types and modalities. The existing design, statistics and leakage tooling carry over almost unchanged. One caveat on provenance before the details. arxiv.org, openreview.net and ojs.aaai.org were blocked in the research session. The Medical SAM3 PDF was read in full through export.arxiv.org, along with the MedSAM-3 (Liu et al.), Lesion-SAM3 and UltraSAM3 PDFs, and the official Medical SAM3 GitHub repo was cloned. Nearly every unlearning-literature detail, however, comes from search excerpts and aggregator summaries, not full texts.

## The current project addresses none of Reproduce, Extend or Contribute

The project's own documents define it as a pre-registered study of whether a "spatial internal audit score measured *before* any perturbation predict[s] whether the corrupted ring re-emerges" in SAM ViT-B fine-tuned on Kvasir-SEG, with SSD, SAP and retrain-minus-flagged as the unlearning methods (`design/DESIGN.md`). Mapped against the card, almost nothing lines up.

| Card element | What the card asks | Current project | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reproduce | Medical SAM3's prompt-driven results on the authors' public benchmark | No Medical SAM3 run, no SAM 3 model, no Table 3 reproduction | **Not met** |
| Extend (modality) | Transfer to ultrasound or dermoscopy | Kvasir-SEG only, with CVC-ClinicDB as the external test | **Not met** |
| Extend (prompt type) | Point vs box vs text | Point and box *perturbations* exist as a recovery probe; SAM ViT-B has no text pathway | **Partly** (points and boxes only, for another purpose) |
| Contribute | Per-modality prompt-selection protocol, failure taxonomy, released code | Re-emergence audit; no protocol, no taxonomy; code is partial | **Not met** |

Four mismatches sit underneath this table. The **model** is SAM v1, while the card's base is SAM 3 with text prompts, 1008 px input and a detector–tracker design. The **data** is in-distribution for the base paper, because Kvasir-SEG (1,000 images) is listed in Medical SAM3's training corpus ([Medical SAM3 PDF, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10880)). The **task** is corrective unlearning of label corruption, which is orthogonal to the card's transfer question. And there is **no reproduction step**. There is one accidental bridge. The project's external test set, CVC-ClinicDB, is one of Medical SAM3's seven external benchmarks, scoring 87.9 Dice with text prompts against 0.0 for vanilla SAM 3 ([Medical SAM3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10880)). The team therefore already holds part of the reproduction data. The fair reading is that the advisor's "unique twist" has become the entire project, and the card has dropped out.

## The card's "untested modality" premise no longer holds

Ultrasound and dermoscopy are not untested for Medical SAM3. The paper fully fine-tunes SAM 3 on **33 public 2D datasets (76,956 images) across 10 modalities**. Seven of those are ultrasound sets totalling 12,179 images, including BUSI, BUS-UCLM and SegThy, and ISIC 2018 (2,594 images) covers dermoscopy. Its held-out external evaluation already includes **TN3K thyroid ultrasound (4.2 → 40.8 Dice), HC18 fetal-head ultrasound (23.9 → 92.6) and PH2 dermoscopy (18.4 → 92.7)** ([Medical SAM3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10880)). Before the PDF was read, one research thread treated BUSI's training status as uncertain from search snippets. The full-text reading settles it: BUSI is in training. Even "external" here means a dataset-level shift inside seen modalities, not a modality-level shift, because sister datasets of the external sets are in training (Kvasir-SEG, PolypGen and BKAI for the polyp sets, ISIC 2018 for PH2). The paper also compares only against vanilla SAM 3, with no MedSAM, BiomedParse or nnU-Net baseline.

The prompt-type axis is equally crowded. Medical SAM3 itself reports **text-only results**, since "for both training and evaluation, we use text-only prompts" ([Medical SAM3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10880)). Its repo's 2D evaluation script does run ground-truth-box prompts, but has no point-prompt path ([Medical SAM3 repo](https://github.com/AIM-Research-Lab/Medical-SAM3)). Other groups have covered the rest. Lesion-SAM3 compares point, box, text and exemplar prompts on BUSI, ISIC 2018 and Kvasir-SEG, with GT-box Dice of 90.82 on ISIC 2018 for SAM 3, and finds text-only prompts "consistently yield the poorest" results ([Xu et al., arXiv 2603.25945](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.25945)). MedSAM-3 (Liu et al.), a different paper with a confusingly similar name, compares text alone against text plus box: BUSI goes from 0 to 0.7110 Dice for vanilla SAM 3, and ISIC 2018 from 0.2189 to 0.8178 ([Liu et al., arXiv 2511.19046](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.19046)). UltraSAM3 benchmarks Medical SAM3 itself on four external ultrasound sets. On KFGNet thyroid, Medical SAM3 scores only **0.1577 Dice** against UltraSAM3's 0.5082 ([UltraSAM3, arXiv 2607.29200](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.29200)). A pathology study already proposes a "systematic evaluation protocol" with prompt guidance for SAM 3 ([Kong et al., arXiv 2604.18225](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18225)). Per-modality prompt choice also dates back to the SAM 1 era ([Cheng et al., arXiv 2305.00035](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00035)), and so do failure-factor analyses such as boundary complexity and intensity contrast ([Huang et al., arXiv 2304.14660](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14660)).

What remains open on the card is narrow. No one has published a point/box/text sweep of the *fine-tuned Medical SAM3 checkpoint* on held-out ultrasound and dermoscopy data, nor a combined decision protocol and image-level failure taxonomy for SAM 3-family medical models. Thyroid ultrasound is the visible weak point. A well-executed card project is still a legitimate contribution, but only an incremental one. That is the real reason the unlearning twist matters: it is where the novelty has to come from.

## The unlearning audit's novelty sits in three specific claims

Most of the conceptual framing around the audit is already published. The table separates what the session's searches found, with the caveat that unlearning-paper details come almost entirely from excerpts.

| Element | Status | Closest prior work |
|---|---|---|
| Unlearning as suppression, not deletion; representation-level restoration audits | **Published** | Jang et al., SAE restoration across 12 methods ([arXiv 2602.18505](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18505v1)); Xu et al., reversible vs irreversible forgetting via CKA/PCA ([arXiv 2505.16831](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16831v2)) |
| Recovery under relearning or benign fine-tuning | **Published** | Siddiqui et al., forget-set accuracy ~50% → ~100% after retain-only fine-tuning ([arXiv 2505.22310](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22310v2)); Hu et al. ([arXiv 2406.13356](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13356v2)) |
| A pre-attack audit predicts recovery (model level) | **Published** | J-Access: partial correlation ~+0.33 over 398 models, explicitly *no item-level prediction* ([Song et al., arXiv 2608.11408](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11408)) |
| Residual knowledge under perturbed inputs | **Published** (classification) | RURK ([NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/2656bba937d78593fbd99ace9f14e311-Abstract-Conference.html)) |
| Corrective unlearning with partial identification | **Published** (classification) | Goel et al.; even 80% identification fails to remove effects of 1% manipulation ([arXiv 2402.14015](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14015v2)) |
| Unlearning in segmentation / medical segmentation | **Partly published** | DELETE, CVPR 2025 ([CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Zhou_Decoupled_Distillation_to_Erase_A_General_Unlearning_Method_for_Any_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)); Erase to Retain on ISIC ([arXiv 2511.16574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16574)); 3D brain MRI ([arXiv 2606.16180](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16180)). All output-level, privacy- or class-motivated |
| Unlearning in SAM-family models | **Partly published** | SCU, concept removal, AAAI 2026 ([AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/39233)) |
| Pixel-level metrics for unlearning | **Partly published** (output space only) | RUB per-pixel L1 ([summary](https://pith.science/paper/2504.14798)); DAMP qualitative maps ([arXiv 2604.15166](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15166)) |
| Corrective unlearning of a spatially structured mask artifact in segmentation | **Not found** | Nearest: segmentation backdoor repair, which needs input triggers ([RePAIR, arXiv 2610.37015](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37015)) |
| Internal, pixel-localized residual audit against a clean reference | **Not found** | — |
| Pixel- or item-level prediction of *where* re-emergence happens | **Not found** | J-Access states it cannot do this |
| Re-emergence under prompt perturbation in promptable segmentation | **Not found** | Prompt fragility of SAM itself is known ([PP-SAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16740v1)) |
| Unlearning efficacy as a function of prompt type or modality | **Not found** | — |
| Unlearning in SAM 3 / Medical SAM3, or modality-level unlearning in a promptable medical model | **Not found** | Continual learning treats forgetting as harm ([SAMed-2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.03698)) |

That leaves three defensible headline claims. The first is spatial localization: residual signal measured at the exact corrupted pixels, with offset and inner-band controls against the boundary confound. The second is item- or pixel-level predictive validity, the gap J-Access names explicitly. The third is prompt-conditional re-emergence in a promptable segmenter. The team must not claim that "an audit predicts relearning" is new in general. J-Access is the direct precedent, and its "measure, don't optimize" result is a warning that audit scores can be gamed if the team ever uses them as a loss ([Song et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11408)). Siddiqui et al.'s weight-space L2 and linear mode connectivity are cheap baselines that the spatial audit has to beat. Pawelczyk et al.'s finding that unlearning fails to remove data poisons gives the strongest motivation for the whole line of work ([arXiv 2406.17216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17216v3)). Two unread full texts carry the main risk. SCU has an attention-suppression module and might contain feature-level analysis or prompt-type results. DAMP might contain quantitative per-region metrics.

## A merged project: prompt-conditional corrective unlearning in Medical SAM3

The recommended direction keeps the card's skeleton and makes unlearning the evaluation lens. The core question becomes: **after corrective unlearning of erroneous masks in Medical SAM3, does the correction hold under text, box and point prompts, and does that differ between ultrasound, dermoscopy and endoscopy?** A mechanism makes this more than a grid search. SAM 3's presence head separates "is the concept here?" from "where is it?" ([SAM 3, arXiv 2511.16719](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.16719)). Medical SAM3 was also trained text-only, while vanilla SAM 3's performance "largely relies" on GT boxes ([Medical SAM3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10880)). So text-conditioned unlearning could look clean while a box prompt routes around the presence head and recovers the corrupted mask. That is a testable form of prompt-conditional residual knowledge, and nothing found covers it.

Each card element then has a concrete task. **Reproduce:** run the released checkpoint through the repo's 2D evaluation on Table 3's external rows. TN3K, HC18, PH2, CVC-ClinicDB and ETIS-Larib are the minimum; CHASE_DB1 and STARE are optional. Pre-specify a tolerance, and also report the GT-box numbers that the script produces but the paper omits. **Extend:** fine-tune onto held-out sets that are absent from Table 1 and Table 3. DDTI or BUS-BRA are candidates for ultrasound, and HAM10000 or IMA++ for dermoscopy, after a near-duplicate check against ISIC 2018. BrEaST is a poor choice, because UltraSAM3 already uses it externally ([UltraSAM3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.29200)). Then sweep point, box (tight, jittered), text (canonical, synonyms) and text+box prompts, with vanilla SAM 3 and ideally MedSAM as baselines. **Twist:** inject mask corruption during this fine-tuning stage. Unlearn it with the existing methods, then run the spatial audit and both recovery arms in every modality × prompt cell. **Contribute:** build a per-modality prompt-selection protocol with an added "robustness after correction" criterion. Pair it with a failure taxonomy that extends Medical SAM3's own categories (near-empty masks, over-segmentation collapse, thin-structure false positives, small-target fragmentation) with unlearning-specific ones: residual leakage, prompt-bypass re-emergence and over-forgetting. Release all of it as code.

Placing the corruption at the fine-tuning stage solves the hardest methodological problem. Retraining Medical SAM3 from scratch needs 4× H100 80 GB and the reassembly of 33 datasets ([Medical SAM3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10880)). The internal 85/15 split files also exist only as seed-42 split logic inside a YAML file with cluster paths ([repo](https://github.com/AIM-Research-Lab/Medical-SAM3)). If every condition starts from the released checkpoint and fine-tunes on the new dataset, the "oracle" is simply the same fine-tune on clean masks. That makes it an exact reference, which preserves the design's Clean, Corrupted, Oracle and Unlearned structure. Real annotation errors can serve as a secondary corruption family, which also answers the design's own "synthetic corruption" threat to validity. BUSI contains duplicate images with conflicting masks ([PMC10293973](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10293973/)). IMA++ tags each ISIC mask by creation tool and annotator skill, so flood-fill or novice masks can be paired with expert masks of the same image ([arXiv 2512.21472](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.21472)). Because BUSI is already in Medical SAM3's training data, though, BUSI-based experiments have no clean oracle and belong in an exploratory arm.

### What to reuse, what to change

Most of the existing work carries over. The statistical frame is model-agnostic and should be kept intact: seed as the unit of replication, randomized complete blocks, nested flag fractions with cheap repeated flagged-subset draws, TOST equivalence for "looks clean", the H1/H2/H3 hierarchy with M0-vs-M1 likelihood-ratio tests, blinding, and the rule against tuning on the audit score. The offset and inner-band specificity controls, the frozen-encoder sanity check, the oracle-referenced re-emergence metric R, and the paired per-image prompt seeds also carry over as written. `src/dedup.py` and `src/make_splits.py` become more valuable, not less, because leakage against Medical SAM3's training corpus (ISIC 2018 vs HAM10000/IMA++, BUSI duplicates) is now a central validity threat. `prompt_conditions.csv` and `recovery_finetune.csv` need extending, not replacing. `power_sim*.py` should be re-run with development-seed estimates, exactly as Section 8 of the design already requires.

Several things have to change. The model becomes Medical SAM3, with vanilla SAM 3 as a reference, and the LoRA-versus-full fine-tuning scope must be fixed in advance. The prompt grid gains text, synonym text and text+box conditions. Point prompts need SAM 3's native interactive path, because the Medical SAM3 eval code has none. A modality factor is added, and the ring band generalizes to an "error band", meaning the pixels where the corrupted and corrected masks differ. The audit gains the literature's baselines: weight-space L2 and linear mode connectivity ([Siddiqui et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22310v2)), J-Access-style partial correlations that control for output-level forgetting ([Song et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11408)), and a segmentation membership-inference attack ([Chobola et al., arXiv 2212.01082](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.01082)). Finally, the scale must shrink. The current plan of 234 confirmatory models was budgeted for SAM ViT-B. With a model at 1008 px and three modalities, the team should cut flag fractions or methods before cutting seeds. The extra inference cost of SAM 3 is an estimate and has not been measured.

### Verify before claiming novelty

Seven checks come first, in this order. First, **read the SCU full text** (AAAI 2026) for its backbone, prompt types and any feature-level or relearning analysis. It is the single largest novelty risk, and its code may be unreleased. Second, **confirm the Hugging Face checkpoint is the 2D v1 model behind Table 3** and resolve the license conflict before promising "released code". The repo ships Meta's SAM License, while a Hugging Face snippet says MIT ([repo](https://github.com/AIM-Research-Lab/Medical-SAM3)). Third, **verify that each candidate held-out dataset** is absent from Medical SAM3's Table 1, from UltraSAM3's external set and, where possible, from SAM 3's SA-Co pretraining. Then run `dedup.py` across the boundaries. Fourth, **read the full texts** of J-Access, DAMP, Erase to Retain, arXiv 2606.16180, RePAIR, the TRAK-for-segmentation submission ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/pdf/e15a148b35fbb56da1af7d88c7c66add68615a6a.pdf)) and "Forget or Fine-tune?" on noisy-label unlearning ([arXiv 2608.30046](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.30046)), whose findings could not be retrieved. Fifth, **run a fresh September to November 2026 sweep** of arXiv listings and MICCAI 2026 main and workshop proceedings for "SAM 3 unlearning", "MedSAM unlearning", "prompt-type unlearning" and "corrective unlearning segmentation". The session's searches were weakest on this window. Sixth, **pilot the positive control (H1)** on the development seed. If fine-tuning Medical SAM3 does not learn the corrupted pattern, there is nothing to unlearn. ADELE also warns that strongly structured noise may skip the usual early-learning phase ([arXiv 2110.03740](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03740)). Seventh, confirm every item marked unverified in the notes before it goes into a bibliography, including ConceptVectors, Pham et al.'s concept inversion and UnlearnDiffAtk.

## Conclusion

The project and the card are failing in opposite directions. The card has lost most of its novelty to a fast-moving 2026 SAM 3 literature, and Medical SAM3 has already tested its "untested" modalities. The team's audit has real novelty, but it answers a question the card never asked. Neither stands well alone, and the merged version is stronger than either. Prompt type and modality are exactly the variables that make residual-knowledge auditing new in segmentation, and corrective unlearning is what makes the card's prompt-selection protocol more than a benchmark table. The headline claim should be narrow and testable: in a text-trained medical promptable model, apparently successful corrective unlearning can be bypassed by geometric prompts, and a pixel-level audit taken before any perturbation predicts where that happens.

The novelty verdict is provisional. It rests on the absence of findings in searches that could not read most unlearning full texts. The SCU paper and the last two months of arXiv and MICCAI output are the places where it could still fail. The team should run those checks before writing any "first to" sentence. That costs a few days, far less than the GPU budget the design already commits.
